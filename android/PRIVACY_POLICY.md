# Privacy Policy for Brain Sticky (脑雾收集站)

**Last updated:** August 24, 2026

Rita Studio ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how **Brain Sticky (脑雾收集站)** handles your information.

## 1. Zero Data Collection
Brain Sticky is designed with a **privacy-first, local-offline architecture**. 
- We **do not collect, store, transmit, or share** any of your personal data, notes, passwords, todos, groceries, or habits.
- All information you enter in Brain Sticky is stored **exclusively on your local device**.
- No account registration or login is required to use the app.

## 2. Permissions & Device Access
Brain Sticky requests only standard local device permissions strictly necessary for its functionality:
- **Notifications / Alarms (Optional)**: Used locally on your device to trigger todo reminders and habit check-in notifications that you set.
- Brain Sticky **does not access** your location, contacts, microphone, camera, or photos.

## 3. Third-Party Services & Analytics
Brain Sticky **does not contain any third-party advertising SDKs, tracking libraries, or analytics frameworks**. 

## 4. Children's Privacy
Because Brain Sticky does not collect any personal data from any user, it is safe for all age groups and compliant with COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act).

## 5. Security of Your Data
All your notes and data remain safely isolated inside your device's private sandbox storage. You have full control to edit, delete, or wipe your data at any time by uninstalling the application.
